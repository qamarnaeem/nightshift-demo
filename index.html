You are the customer. Press Start call, wait for it to connect, say "Hello?" and answer the bot like a real prospect. Use Chrome and, if possible, earphones.

Things to try
  1. Pick a script at the top (Final Expense, Medicare Benefits, Auto Accident) before you press Start call.
  2. Happy path: Hello? → yes → yes, go ahead → yes → no → warm transfer.
  3. Interrupt it: while it is pitching, say how much does it cost? It stops mid-sentence, answers, and continues.
  4. Objections: I already have insurance, is this a scam?, send me something in the mail, I can't afford it, I need to ask my wife.
  5. Off-script question: what's the weather like there? One fallback line, then it exits. It never improvises.
  6. Voicemail: at the start say Hi, you've reached Robert, leave a message after the tone. The bot detects the machine and drops the call.
  7. DNC: take me off your list.
  8. Hold, repeat, silence: say wait, hold on and come back with okay, go ahead. Say what? or say that again. Say something random once, it re-asks; twice, it closes. Say nothing: it re-asks, checks the line, then drops.
  9. Difficult customers: refuse the age question with why do you need my age?, say my son has power of attorney, get angry with I'm sick of these calls, or say my husband passed away. It calms, never argues.
