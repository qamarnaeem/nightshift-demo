You are the customer. Press Start call, wait for it to connect, say "Hello?" and answer the bot like a real prospect. Use Chrome and, if possible, earphones.

Test calls are saved as text (no audio) to keep improving the bot.

Use headphones, or keep the speaker low, so the mic hears you and not the bot. A real phone line keeps the two audio paths separate, so this only matters in the browser demo.
Things to try
  1. Pick a script (Final Expense, Medicare Benefits, Auto Accident, Debt Relief, Solar) and an agent (six American voices) before you press Start call.
  2. Happy path: Hello? → yes → yes, go ahead → yes → no → warm transfer.
  3. Interrupt it: while it is pitching, say how much does it cost? It stops mid-word, answers, and continues.
  4. Nod along: say uh-huh, yeah yeah or mm-hm while it talks: it keeps going. Say huh? or sorry, what? mid-sentence: it says that sentence again. Say something random mid-pitch: it reacts, then picks up where it left off.
  5. Objections: I already have insurance, is this a scam?, send me something in the mail, I can't afford it, I need to ask my wife.
  6. Off-script question: what's the weather like there? One fallback line, then it exits. It never improvises.
  7. Voicemail: at the start say Hi, you've reached Robert, leave a message after the tone. The bot detects the machine and drops the call.
  8. DNC: take me off your list.
  9. Hold, repeat, silence: say wait, hold on and come back with okay, go ahead. Say what? or say that again. Say something random once, it re-asks; twice, it closes. Say nothing: it re-asks, checks the line, then drops.
  10. Difficult customers: refuse the age question with why do you need my age?, say my son has power of attorney, get angry with I'm sick of these calls, or say my husband passed away. It calms, never argues.
